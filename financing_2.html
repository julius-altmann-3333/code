🏠 Traditional Financing Options

Conventional Mortgage Loans

  • Fixed-rate or adjustable-rate
  • Typically require 5–20% down
  • Best for buyers with strong credit and steady income

FHA Loans

  • Low down payment (as low as 3.5%)
  • Ideal for first-time buyers or those with less-than-perfect credit

VA Loans

  • Zero down payment and no PMI
  • Only for U.S. military veterans and families

USDA Loans

  • For rural/suburban homes
  • Low to no down payment

Jumbo Loans

  • For homes over ~$766,550 (2024 conforming limit)
  • Requires strong credit and larger down payment

💼 Investment Property Financing

  • Hard Money Loans – Short-term, based on asset value, higher rates
  • Private Money Loans – Individual lenders, flexible terms
  • Commercial Real Estate Loans – Office, retail, apartments
  • Portfolio Loans – Held by lender, not resold

🏗️ Development & Construction Financing

  • Construction Loans – Short-term for building
  • Bridge Loans – Short-term gap financing
  • Mezzanine Financing – Debt + equity hybrid

🧰 Creative & Alternative Financing

  • Seller Financing – Seller acts as lender
  • Lease Option – Rent-to-own with purchase option
  • Subject-To Financing – Take over seller’s mortgage
  • Real Estate Syndication – Group investing
  • Crowdfunding – Online investing in real estate

💸 Top Financing Options for Buying a Mansion

  • Jumbo Loan – For high-value homes
  • Portfolio Loan – Flexible, stays with lender
  • Asset-Based Loan – Based on investments/assets
  • Private Bank Mortgage – Premium service for high-net-worth buyers
  • Seller Financing – Flexible private agreement

🧠 Smart Extras to Consider

  • Interest-Only Mortgages
  • HELOC for down payment
  • Trust or LLC for ownership structure
