Plane Manufacturers and Models

Boeing (United States)

  • 737 Series
  • 747 Series
  • 767 Series
  • 777 Series
  • 787 Dreamliner

Current Production: Boeing continues to produce various models, with a focus on the 737 MAX, 787 Dreamliner, and 777X.

Airbus (France/Germany/Spain/United Kingdom)

  • A220 Series
  • A320 Family
  • A330 Series
  • A350 XWB

Current Production: Airbus continues to manufacture the A320, A330, and A350 families, with a significant focus on the A320neo and A350 XWB models.

Embraer (Brazil)

  • E-Jet Series

Current Production: Embraer continues to produce the E-Jet series, with the E2 variants offering improved efficiency and passenger comfort.

Bombardier (Canada)

  • CRJ Series

Current Production: Bombardier has ceased production of the CRJ series and focuses on business jets like the Global and Challenger families.

United Aircraft Corporation (UAC) (Russia)

  • Sukhoi Superjet 100
  • Irkut MC-21

Current Production: Production of the Sukhoi Superjet 100 continues, while the MC-21 is undergoing certification with deliveries expected in the near future.

ATR (France/Italy)

  • ATR 42
  • ATR 72

Current Production: ATR continues to produce the ATR 42 and ATR 72 models, with a focus on enhancing efficiency and performance.

De Havilland Canada (Canada)

  • Dash 8 Series

Current Production: Production of the Dash 8 series continues, with a focus on the Q400 model.

Mitsubishi Aircraft Corporation (Japan)

  • SpaceJet Series

Current Production: The SpaceJet program has faced delays, with ongoing efforts to achieve certification.

COMAC (China)

  • C919

Current Production: The C919 is undergoing testing, with deliveries anticipated in the coming years.
