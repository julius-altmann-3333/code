Your First Home in Germany

Tailored guides for millennials, single buyers, and young families in Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Dresden, Leipzig, and Chemnitz.

Berlin

Millennials

  • Hotspots: Neukölln, Friedrichshain, Kreuzberg
  • Avg. price: ~€5,050/m²

Single Buyers

  • Trendy districts: Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg
  • Compact, stylish apartments

Young Families

  • Suburbs: Pankow, Köpenick
  • Access to parks, daycare, and schools

Hamburg

Millennials

  • Up-and-coming: Sternschanze, St. Pauli
  • Nightlife, culture, startup scene

Single Buyers

  • City access: Eimsbüttel, Ottensen
  • Avg. price: ~€5,800/m²

Young Families

  • Quiet areas: Blankenese, Winterhude
  • Known for great schools and safety

Munich (München)

Millennials

  • Popular: Maxvorstadt, Glockenbachviertel
  • Young professionals and vibrant lifestyle

Single Buyers

  • Well-connected: Schwabing, Haidhausen
  • Premium but accessible locations

Young Families

  • Family zones: Bogenhausen, Aubing
  • Parks, schools, and community vibes

Dresden

Millennials

  • Creative zones: Neustadt, Friedrichstadt
  • Affordable rents, artistic scene

Single Buyers

  • Central living: Altstadt
  • Historic views, cultural offerings

Young Families

  • Recommended: Striesen, Blasewitz
  • Green neighborhoods, family-centric services

Leipzig

Millennials

  • Trendy: Plagwitz, Südvorstadt
  • Startup scene and affordable pricing (~€3,000/m²)

Single Buyers

  • Central: Zentrum, Gohlis
  • Walkable, connected, buzzing with cafés

Young Families

  • Calm areas: Schleußig, Lindenau
  • Great parks and childcare facilities

Chemnitz

Millennials

  • Reviving districts: Sonnenberg, Kaßberg
  • Creative projects, low entry cost

Single Buyers

  • Compact living: Zentrum, Schloßchemnitz
  • Affordable housing and solid returns

Young Families

  • Family focus: Rabenstein, Hutholz
  • Quiet streets and modern housing

General Tips

  • Secure mortgage pre-approval
  • Include notary early in process
  • Prepare for closing costs (10% extra)
  • Use local estate agents for area expertise
