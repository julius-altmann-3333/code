Pinterest SEO Funnel Guide for Real Estate

Your Pinterest strategy for different real estate buyer and investor groups

Residential Buyers

1. First-time Homebuyers

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Create infographic pins with homebuying tips for first-time buyers. - Design beautiful pins showcasing affordable homes for first-time buyers.
  • Pin Titles: - “Top 10 First-time Homebuyer Tips for Success” - “Affordable Homes for First-Time Buyers in [City]”
  • Boards: - Create boards such as "First-Time Homebuying Tips" and "Affordable Homes."
  • Hashtags: - #FirstTimeHomebuyer #HomeLoanTips #AffordableHomes

2. Move-up Buyers

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Pins that highlight homes with large yards or extra bedrooms. - Pins about the best neighborhoods for families to upgrade.
  • Pin Titles: - “Top 5 Tips for Moving Up to Your Dream Home” - “Best Neighborhoods for Families in [City]”
  • Boards: - “Move-Up Homes” and “Family-Friendly Neighborhoods”
  • Hashtags: - #MoveUpBuyer #FamilyHomes #UpgradingYourHome

3. Luxury Homebuyers

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - High-quality images of luxurious homes with pools, views, and exclusive features. - Pins that highlight the top luxury neighborhoods or gated communities.
  • Pin Titles: - “Explore Luxury Homes with Stunning Views” - “The Best Gated Communities for Luxury Living in [City]”
  • Boards: - “Luxury Homes” and “Gated Communities”
  • Hashtags: - #LuxuryHomes #GatedCommunities #HighEndLiving

4. Downsizers/Empty Nesters

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Pins that feature smaller homes like condos and townhomes, perfect for retirees. - Create boards showcasing low-maintenance properties ideal for downsizing.
  • Pin Titles: - “The Best Homes for Downsizing and Retiring in [City]” - “Top Low-Maintenance Homes for Empty Nesters”
  • Boards: - “Downsizing Tips” and “Retirement Homes”
  • Hashtags: - #Downsizing #RetirementHomes #EmptyNestLiving

5. Vacation Home Buyers

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Pins showcasing beach, mountain, and resort-style vacation homes. - Create “best places to buy a second home” pins with images and details.
  • Pin Titles: - “Vacation Homes in [Location]: Your Dream Getaway Awaits” - “Best Places to Buy a Second Home for Vacation Rental”
  • Boards: - “Vacation Homes” and “Second Home Investment”
  • Hashtags: - #VacationHome #SecondHome #InvestmentProperty

6. Multigenerational Buyers

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Pins showcasing homes with separate living spaces or in-law suites. - Create pins highlighting family-friendly features for multigenerational homes.
  • Pin Titles: - “Multigenerational Homes with Separate Living Spaces” - “Best Neighborhoods for Multigenerational Families”
  • Boards: - “Multigenerational Homes” and “Family Homes”
  • Hashtags: - #MultigenerationalHomes #InLawSuites #FamilyHomes

Real Estate Investors

1. Fix-and-flip Investors

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Before-and-after pins of flipped properties. - Infographic pins about flipping homes for profit.
  • Pin Titles: - “Flipping Houses for Profit: Top Tips” - “Before and After: Real Estate Flipping Success Stories”
  • Boards: - “Fix-and-Flip Tips” and “Real Estate Investing”
  • Hashtags: - #FixAndFlip #RealEstateInvesting #PropertyFlipping

2. Buy-and-hold Investors

Pinterest Funnel Strategy:

  • Pin Ideas: - Create pins highlighting rental properties that generate consistent income. - Infographics showing long-term wealth building through real estate.
  • Pin Titles: - “How to Start Building Wealth with Buy-and-Hold Properties” - “Top Neighborhoods for Rental Income Investment”
  • Boards: - “Rental Property Tips” and “Real Estate Investing”
  • Hashtags: - #BuyAndHold #RealEstateInvestment #PassiveIncome
