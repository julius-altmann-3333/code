Real Estate Buyer Groups

Residential Buyers

1. First-time Homebuyers

Demographics:

  • Age: 25-35 years old
  • Income: $50,000 - $100,000 per year
  • Looking for affordable homes in urban or suburban areas
  • Likely to qualify for first-time homebuyer programs
  • Typically seeking smaller properties (1-3 bedrooms) and starter homes

Search Terms:

  • Best homes for first-time buyers
  • Affordable homes for first-time buyers in [city]
  • First-time homebuyer programs
  • Tips for buying my first home
  • How to qualify for a first-time home loan

2. Move-up Buyers

Demographics:

  • Age: 35-50 years old
  • Income: $80,000 - $150,000 per year
  • Looking for larger homes, often with multiple bedrooms and a bigger yard
  • May have growing families or desire more space for personal needs
  • Typically have an existing home to sell to finance the purchase of the new home

Search Terms:

  • Upgrading to a bigger home
  • Homes with large yards in [city]
  • Move-up buyer home loan options
  • Best neighborhoods for family homes
  • What to look for when upgrading your home

3. Luxury Homebuyers

Demographics:

  • Age: 40-65 years old
  • Income: $150,000+ per year
  • Looking for high-end, luxurious homes with premium amenities
  • Interested in exclusive neighborhoods and gated communities
  • Often looking for investment properties or vacation homes

Search Terms:

  • Luxury homes for sale in [city/region]
  • High-end homes with pools and views
  • Best areas to buy luxury properties
  • Luxury home investment opportunities
  • Top luxury real estate agents in [location]

4. Downsizers/Empty Nesters

Demographics:

  • Age: 55+ years old
  • Income: $70,000 - $120,000 per year
  • Looking for smaller homes with minimal upkeep
  • Often in search of single-story homes, condos, or townhomes
  • Looking for homes in quieter, more relaxed environments, often near amenities for retirees

Search Terms:

  • Smaller homes for sale near [location]
  • Retirement homes in [city/region]
  • Homes with no maintenance for retirees
  • Best places for downsizing in retirement
  • Affordable homes for empty nesters

5. Vacation Home Buyers

Demographics:

  • Age: 35-60 years old
  • Income: $100,000 - $250,000 per year
  • Looking for vacation homes or second homes in desirable, often resort-style areas
  • May be seeking properties to generate income through vacation rentals
  • Often interested in beach or mountain properties

Search Terms:

  • Vacation homes for sale in [location]
  • Best places to buy a second home
  • Income-generating vacation properties
  • How to find a second home for vacation rental
  • Best investment properties in popular vacation spots

6. Multigenerational Buyers

Demographics:

  • Age: 40-70 years old
  • Income: $80,000 - $150,000 per year
  • Looking for homes with multiple bedrooms, separate living spaces, or additional suites
  • Interested in living with or near parents, children, or extended family
  • Seek properties in family-friendly neighborhoods with access to good schools

Search Terms:

  • Homes with separate living spaces
  • Multi-generational homes for sale
  • Buying a home for multiple generations
  • Homes with in-law suites
  • Best neighborhoods for multigenerational families

Real Estate Investors

1. Fix-and-flip Investors

Demographics:

  • Age: 30-50 years old
  • Income: Varies, typically based on the number of properties bought and sold
  • Looking for distressed properties to renovate and sell for profit
  • Highly interested in the property’s location, cost of renovation, and potential ROI

Search Terms:

  • Best real estate markets for investment
  • How to get started with real estate investing
  • Best properties for flipping
  • Commercial real estate investment opportunities
  • How to invest in rental properties

2. Buy-and-hold Investors

Demographics:

  • Age: 35-55 years old
  • Income: $75,000+ per year
  • Seeking properties that generate consistent rental income
  • Investing in single-family homes, multi-family units, or commercial properties
  • Interested in long-term wealth-building strategies through real estate

Search Terms:

  • Real estate rental properties for sale
  • Buy-and-hold investment strategies
  • Best neighborhoods for rental income
  • Multi-family homes for rental investment
  • How to finance a rental property
