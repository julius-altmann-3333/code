How to Qualify for a First-Time Home Loan

Qualifying for your first home loan is easier than you might think. Here are the basic steps to ensure you're eligible:

  • Check your credit score – most first-time home loans require a score of 620 or higher.
  • Ensure stable employment and income – most lenders want to see 2+ years of steady income.
  • Save for a down payment – most programs require 3-5% down.
  • Be prepared to provide documentation, such as tax returns and bank statements.
