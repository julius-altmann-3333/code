Real Estate-Specific Lead Generation Platforms

  • Zillow Premier Agent – Paid advertising on Zillow to get exclusive buyer/seller leads.
  • Realtor.com Connections Plus – Generates high-intent leads through Realtor.com.
  • Redfin Partner Program – Connects realtors with buyers and sellers.
  • Homesnap Pro+ – Helps agents get leads and improve SEO.
  • BoldLeads – Automated lead generation and follow-up for real estate agents.
  • Placester – Provides websites with built-in lead capture and CRM.
  • Real Geeks – Website, CRM, and automated marketing for real estate lead generation.
  • Market Leader – Paid lead generation with exclusive buyer and seller leads.
  • Zurple – AI-driven lead generation and follow-up platform.
  • kvCORE – A powerful real estate CRM with lead gen and automation.

Classifieds & Local Directories

  • Craigslist – Post rental or FSBO (For Sale By Owner) listings for free.
  • Oodle – A lesser-known but effective classifieds site.
  • Facebook Marketplace – Great for local rental and real estate listings.
  • Nextdoor – Hyperlocal platform for community-based real estate marketing.
  • Angi (Angie’s List) – Good for targeting homeowners needing real estate services.
  • Yelp – Helps real estate agents build trust and generate leads.
  • Houzz – A niche platform for targeting homeowners and real estate investors.

Pay-Per-Lead & Referral Networks

  • UpNest – Matches home sellers with real estate agents.
  • FastExpert – Connects agents with buyers and sellers.
  • HomeLight – Matches buyers and sellers with real estate professionals.
  • Agent Pronto – Helps connect agents with serious home buyers and sellers.
  • OpCity (Realtor.com Leads) – Lead referral program through Realtor.com.

SEO & Blogging for Organic Lead Generation

  • BiggerPockets – Ideal for real estate investors and networking.
  • Quora & Reddit – Answering real estate-related questions can establish credibility and attract leads.
  • Medium & Substack – Publish blog posts about real estate trends and home-buying tips.
  • Google My Business – Helps with local SEO and attracts leads directly from search.
  • LinkedIn Articles – Writing real estate insights can attract high-end buyers and investors.
