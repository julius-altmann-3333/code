Google Search Term Targeting for Real Estate

1. Types of Search Terms to Target

A. Informational Keywords

  • "How to buy a home for the first time"
  • "Best real estate investments in [City]"

B. Transactional Keywords

  • "Homes for sale in [City]"
  • "Sell my house fast in [City]"

2. Keyword Match Types in Google Ads

✔ Broad Match: Shows ads for related searches.

✔ Phrase Match: Shows ads for searches that include your phrase.

✔ Exact Match: Shows ads only when the exact keyword is searched.

3. Negative Keywords to Avoid

  • ❌ "Free real estate listings"
  • ❌ "DIY home selling tips"
  • ❌ "Real estate agent salary"

4. Landing Page Optimization for Search Traffic

  • ✔ Clear CTA: "Schedule a Free Consultation"
  • ✔ Fast Loading Speed: Under 3 seconds
  • ✔ Mobile-Friendly: Optimized for mobile users
  • ✔ Trust Signals: Testimonials, reviews, badges

5. Example Google Ad Strategy

Campaign: "Buy a Home in Miami – Move-In Ready Listings"

Headline 1: "New Homes for Sale in Miami"

Headline 2: "Get Expert Home-Buying Advice"

Description: "Find the perfect home in Miami. View new listings, compare prices, and schedule a free consultation today!"

CTA: "Browse Listings"
