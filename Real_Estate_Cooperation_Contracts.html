Real Estate Sales Cooperation Contract

This agreement is made on [DATE] between:

  • Party A (Real Estate Seller/Broker): [Name & Address]
  • Party B (Cooperating Agent/Broker): [Name & Address]

1. Purpose: This contract establishes the terms under which Party A agrees to cooperate with Party B in selling a real estate property.

2. Property Details: [Property Address, Type, Size, Listing Price]

3. Commission & Payment: Party B shall receive [Percentage]% of the total commission upon successful completion of the transaction.

4. Responsibilities:

  • Party A provides accurate property details, necessary documents, and access for showings.
  • Party B markets the property, finds potential buyers, and facilitates negotiations.

5. Listing Agreement: Party A grants Party B non-exclusive rights to market and sell the property.

6. Duration: This agreement remains valid until [Expiration Date] or until the property is sold.

7. Termination: Either party may terminate the contract with [Number] days' written notice.

8. Confidentiality: Both parties agree to keep all transaction details and client information confidential.

9. Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising from this contract will be resolved through mediation or legal proceedings in [Jurisdiction].

Real Estate Website Sales Cooperation Contract

This agreement is made on [DATE] between:

  • Party A (Website Owner/Publisher): [Company Name & Address]
  • Party B (Real Estate Agency/Agent): [Company Name & Address]

1. Purpose: This contract establishes the terms for Party B to advertise and sell real estate listings through Party A’s website.

2. Listing & Advertising: Party B will provide property listings with descriptions, images, and prices to be displayed on Party A’s website.

3. Compensation: Party B agrees to pay Party A [Flat Fee / Commission-Based Payment] per successful lead or sale generated.

4. Responsibilities:

  • Party A ensures listings remain visible and updates content as necessary.
  • Party B provides high-quality property data, handles inquiries, and manages sales.

5. Lead Generation: Party A will provide lead information (contact details, inquiries) directly to Party B.

6. Contract Duration: [Start Date] to [End Date], with renewal options.

7. Content Ownership & Rights: Party A retains the right to remove or modify listings if required.

8. Confidentiality: Both parties agree to protect user data and maintain compliance with privacy laws.

9. Termination: Either party may terminate this contract with [Number] days' written notice.

10. Legal Jurisdiction: Any disputes will be handled in accordance with the laws of [Jurisdiction].
